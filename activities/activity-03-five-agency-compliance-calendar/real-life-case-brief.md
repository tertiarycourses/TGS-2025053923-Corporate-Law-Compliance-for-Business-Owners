# Real-Life Case Brief: Wan Lin Healthcare Centre

**Regulator:** Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS)
**Published:** 12 December 2022
**Procedural status:** Convictions and sentences reported
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Lim Meng Fatt and Gan Bee Bee were convicted for income-tax and GST evasions involving Wan Lin Healthcare Centre Pte Ltd.
- The conduct included omitted output tax and fraudulent GST deregistration while the company remained liable to be registered.
- The case also included a false Productivity and Innovation Credit claim.

## Classroom Boundary

The tax failures are case facts. ACRA, employment/CPF, WSH and PDPA calendar entries are prospective control extensions and must not be described as breaches found in this case.
