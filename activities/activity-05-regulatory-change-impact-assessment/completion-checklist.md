# Completion Checklist: RORC Change Impact

- [ ] Verify the entity's actual exemption status against current ACRA guidance.
- [ ] Reconcile the annual-return declaration to the underlying RORC evidence.
- [ ] Map impacts across people, process, technology/data, third parties and cost.
- [ ] Document who prepares, checks, approves and files.
- [ ] Require evidence for eligibility rather than relying on a repeated prior-year answer.
- [ ] Compare at least two compliant implementation options.
- [ ] State residual risk, owner, milestones and verification method.
- [ ] Separate current-guidance requirements from offence-period case facts.
