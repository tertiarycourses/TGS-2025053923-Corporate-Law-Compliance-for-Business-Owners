# Real-Life Case Brief: Yuan Shuhua and False RORC Declarations

**Regulator:** Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA)
**Published:** 31 October 2025
**Procedural status:** Convicted, fined and disqualified
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Yuan Shuhua made false declarations in RH Corporate Pte Ltd's annual returns for financial years ended 31 December 2020 and 2021.
- The returns stated that the company was exempt from keeping a Register of Registrable Controllers when it was not.
- He was fined $15,000 and disqualified from company management for five years.

## Classroom Boundary

The conviction is the real-life failure event. Learners must verify current RORC requirements and must not assume today's deadlines were identical during the offence period.
