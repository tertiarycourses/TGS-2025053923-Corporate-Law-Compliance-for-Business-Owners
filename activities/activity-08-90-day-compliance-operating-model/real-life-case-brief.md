# Real-Life Case Brief: Stars Engrg Tuas Explosion

**Regulator:** Ministry of Manpower (MOM)
**Published:** 25 June 2026
**Procedural status:** Director and production manager sentenced; company fined $500,000
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- A 24 February 2021 explosion at Stars Engrg's Tuas worksite killed three workers and injured seven.
- The mixer was operated despite overheating, oil leaks, smoke and an earlier fire.
- Investigations identified failures in risk assessment, safe work procedures, maintenance, supervision and training.
- The production manager was imprisoned for obstructing justice after messages were deleted.

## Classroom Boundary

The published failures are the diagnostic baseline. The 30-60-90 day plan is a learner-designed operating model, not MOM's prescribed remediation plan.
