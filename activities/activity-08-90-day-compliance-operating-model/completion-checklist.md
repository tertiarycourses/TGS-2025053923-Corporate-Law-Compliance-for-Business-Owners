# Completion Checklist: 30-60-90 Compliance Model

- [ ] Define immediate stop-work and hazard-escalation authority.
- [ ] Preserve messages, logs, inspection evidence and incident records.
- [ ] Stabilise risk assessment, safe work procedures, maintenance, supervision and training.
- [ ] Assign board/executive sponsorship and operational accountability.
- [ ] Create a central calendar, issue register and overdue escalation path.
- [ ] Include protected advice/speak-up channels and anti-retaliation controls.
- [ ] Use leading measures such as overdue critical actions, not training counts alone.
- [ ] Separate the learner roadmap from MOM's published findings.
