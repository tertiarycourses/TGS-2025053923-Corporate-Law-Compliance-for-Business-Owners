# Activity 1: Board Duties and Governance Gap Scan

**Mapping:** K1, A1 / LO1
**Duration:** 25 minutes
**Deliverable:** One-page governance gap scan and two-minute board briefing.

## Goal

Identify director, record, reporting and register gaps; distinguish tasks that may be outsourced from accountability that remains with directors.

## Real Case Study

**Case:** Robert Sinpraseuth - false director-appointment filings
**Date:** 18 May 2026
**Status:** Convicted and sentenced in the State Courts
**Official source:** [ACRA - Individual fined $108,000 for authorising false filings](https://www.acra.gov.sg/news-events/news-announcements/an-individual-fined--108-000-for-authorising-false-filings-with-the-registrar-of-companies-under-the-companies-act--cap--50--2006-rev--ed--/)

### Published Facts

ACRA reported that Robert Sinpraseuth authorised a corporate service provider to lodge forms across 12 companies falsely declaring that an individual had consented to be appointed as director. He was convicted on 12 charges and fined a total of $108,000; 12 similar charges were considered for sentencing.

### Exercise Boundary

Use only the published ACRA facts as case evidence. The control redesign and board response are learner recommendations, not facts found by the court.

## Questions

1. What should an authorising person have verified before the 12 filings?
2. Which consent, review and approval evidence should have existed?
3. What immediate review should the affected companies undertake?
4. What maker-checker and director-oversight controls would prevent recurrence?

## Detailed Step-by-Step

1. Read the official case summary once for the chronology and again for the control failures.
2. Create four columns: Requirement, Current fact, Gap/risk, Evidence needed.
3. Map each fact to ACRA director, financial-reporting or register guidance.
4. Classify each response as contain now, correct within deadline, or strengthen for future cycles.
5. Prepare a two-minute board briefing that states the issue, decision, owner and deadline.
6. Peer-review another group's response for unsupported assumptions or missing director challenge.
