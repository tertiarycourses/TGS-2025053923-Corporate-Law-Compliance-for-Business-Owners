# Completion Checklist: Director Filing Governance

- [ ] Confirm the proposed director's identity and written consent before filing.
- [ ] Record who prepared, checked, authorised and lodged each filing.
- [ ] Reconcile filing data to signed source records.
- [ ] Escalate any inconsistency before submission.
- [ ] Separate the service provider's task from directors' continuing accountability.
- [ ] Assign an owner and deadline for correcting affected records.
- [ ] Retain consent, review, approval and lodgement evidence.
- [ ] Label learner assumptions and recommendations separately from case facts.
