# Real-Life Case Brief: Robert Sinpraseuth

**Regulator:** Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA)
**Published:** 18 May 2026
**Procedural status:** Convicted and sentenced in the State Courts
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Robert Sinpraseuth authorised a corporate service provider to lodge false director-appointment filings across 12 companies.
- The filings falsely declared that an individual had consented to act as director.
- He was convicted on 12 charges and fined $108,000; 12 similar charges were considered for sentencing.

## Classroom Boundary

These facts are the real-life case anchor. Any proposed maker-checker workflow, board response, deadline or remediation plan is a learner recommendation, not a court finding.
