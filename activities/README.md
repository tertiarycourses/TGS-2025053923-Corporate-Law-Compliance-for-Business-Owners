# Activities | Corporate Law Compliance for Business Owners

Course: TGS-2025053923 | TSC: ACC-BLR-5001-1.1

Detailed procedures are intentionally kept here and in the Learner Guide, not in the slide deck. Every activity uses a named, published Singapore regulator case. Each folder separates regulator-published facts from learner-created assumptions and recommendations.

Each folder contains:

- `activity-workbook.md` — detailed facilitation and worksheet instructions
- `real-life-case-brief.md` — case identity, procedural status, verified facts and official source
- `completion-checklist.md` — activity-specific quality and evidence checks
- `activity-pack.pdf` — printable combined pack
