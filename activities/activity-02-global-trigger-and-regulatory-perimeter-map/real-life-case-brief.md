# Real-Life Case Brief: 35 Singapore Companies and Foreign Agents

**Regulators:** ACRA and Commercial Affairs Department
**Published:** 1 June 2023
**Procedural status:** Charges and investigation findings reported; allegations are not convictions
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Foreign agents used overseas corporate service providers to engage Singapore providers to incorporate local companies and open bank accounts.
- ACRA reported that nominee directors of 35 Singapore-registered companies allowed foreign agents to operate accounts.
- The accounts received about US$36 million from local and overseas scam victims.
- Twelve persons were charged with Companies Act offences.

## Classroom Boundary

Do not describe the charged persons as convicted. The jurisdiction map, red flags and escalation rules are preventive learner proposals.
