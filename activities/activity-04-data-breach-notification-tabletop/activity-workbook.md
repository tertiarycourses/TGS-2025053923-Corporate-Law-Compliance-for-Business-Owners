# Activity 4: Data Breach Notification Tabletop

**Mapping:** K1, A1 / LO1
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Deliverable:** Incident decision log, notification outline and remediation list.

## Goal

Practise containment, assessment, decision ownership, notification timing and evidence capture under the PDPA breach-notification framework.

## Real Case Study

**Case:** SESAMi (Singapore) Pte Ltd and Abecha Pte Ltd - shared B2B e-commerce ransomware incident
**Date:** 26 February 2026
**Status:** PDPC breach decision and directions
**Official source:** [PDPC - Breach of the Protection Obligation by SESAMi and Abecha](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/all-commissions-decisions/2026/02/breach-of-the-protection-obligation-by-sesami-singapore-pte-ltd-and-abecha-pte-ltd)

### Published Facts

PDPC published a decision concerning a ransomware incident affecting SESAMi and Abecha on a shared B2B e-commerce network. About 39,000 individuals were affected and bank and credit-card details were among the data rendered inaccessible. PDPC identified unpatched systems, weak access controls and failure to enforce multi-factor authentication, found a breach of the Protection Obligation, and issued directions to strengthen security.

### Exercise Boundary

The published materials do not supply every incident-response timestamp needed for this classroom tabletop. Learners must mark scenario assumptions as assumptions and must not present invented timestamps as case facts.

## Questions

1. What containment and evidence-preservation actions should begin first?
2. Which published facts affect significant-harm or significant-scale assessment?
3. Which missing timestamps are needed to apply the three-calendar-day rule?
4. What should the decision log show even when the public summary is silent?

## Detailed Step-by-Step

1. Assign incident roles: coordinator, technical lead, DPO/legal, communications and executive sponsor.
2. List immediate containment actions without assuming deletion is proven.
3. Build a fact log separating known, unknown and evidence required.
4. Apply the notifiability test and document the decision owner.
5. Draft a six-line PDPC notification outline and an affected-person message outline.
6. Record remediation and vendor-control improvements for the post-incident review.
