# Completion Checklist: Data Breach Tabletop

- [ ] Open an incident fact log with known, unknown and assumed fields.
- [ ] Preserve logs, images, access records and the decision chronology.
- [ ] Contain access without destroying forensic evidence.
- [ ] Assess significant harm and significant scale against current PDPC guidance.
- [ ] Record when the organisation first had credible grounds to believe a breach occurred.
- [ ] Name the notifiability decision owner and evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Draft PDPC and affected-person communications without unsupported certainty.
- [ ] Label every classroom timestamp or fact not published by PDPC as an assumption.
