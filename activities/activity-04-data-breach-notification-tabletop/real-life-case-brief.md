# Real-Life Case Brief: SESAMi and Abecha Ransomware Incident

**Regulator:** Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
**Published:** 26 February 2026
**Procedural status:** Breach decision and directions
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- A ransomware incident affected SESAMi (Singapore) Pte Ltd and Abecha Pte Ltd on a shared B2B e-commerce network.
- About 39,000 individuals were affected; bank and credit-card details were among data rendered inaccessible.
- PDPC identified unpatched systems, weak access controls and failure to enforce multi-factor authentication.
- PDPC found a breach of the Protection Obligation and issued directions to strengthen security.
