# Real-Life Case Brief: Marina Bay Sands Software Migration

**Regulator:** Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
**Published:** 28 October 2025
**Procedural status:** Breach decision and $315,000 financial penalty
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Personal data of 665,495 Marina Bay Sands patrons was illegally accessed and exfiltrated in October 2023.
- During a large software migration, one identifier for the Art Science Friends webpage was omitted from security-policy coverage.
- A single employee manually compiled API configurations without a second-layer check; the omission remained undetected for six months.
- PDPC imposed a $315,000 financial penalty.
