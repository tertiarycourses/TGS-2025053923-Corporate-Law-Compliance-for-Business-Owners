# Completion Checklist: Digital Filing Board Decision

- [ ] State the exact board decision requested.
- [ ] Verify supplier identity, authority and transaction substance.
- [ ] Match invoice, purchase order, receipt/delivery and payment evidence.
- [ ] Flag duplicate, unusual, round-dollar or unsupported transactions.
- [ ] Keep human approval for exceptions and input-tax claims.
- [ ] Compare manual improvement, phased adoption and full adoption fairly.
- [ ] Define access, segregation, audit-log, continuity and post-implementation tests.
- [ ] Make clear that automation does not validate a fabricated transaction.
