# Real-Life Case Brief: Nagore Trading GST Fraud

**Regulator:** Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS)
**Published:** 29 June 2026
**Procedural status:** Key syndicate member convicted and sentenced
**Verified:** 12 August 2026

## Regulator-Published Facts

- Giam Zi Hin, Luke was sentenced to six years' imprisonment for fraudulent trading in a GST missing-trader-fraud scheme.
- The scheme involved about S$114 million in fictitious sales.
- Nagore Trading Pte Ltd used forged invoices from at least 12 local suppliers to create a false appearance of purchases and GST payments.

## Classroom Boundary

The case demonstrates that digital filing cannot prove transaction authenticity. Proposed automated controls and board approval conditions are learner recommendations.
