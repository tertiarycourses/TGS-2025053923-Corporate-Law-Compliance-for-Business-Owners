# Activity 6: Board Decision Memo: Digital Filing Controls

**Mapping:** K2, A2 / LO2  
**Duration:** 20 minutes  
**Deliverable:** One-page board decision memo.

## Goal

Frame a proportionate board decision that balances legal compliance, control quality, cost, implementation risk and operational benefit.

## Scenario

The finance director proposes #SFFS-enabled accounting and payroll software. Operations prefers spreadsheets because the software subscription costs more, while the board is concerned about authorisation, data quality and reliance on one vendor.

## Questions

1. What decision is required and by when?
2. What are the viable compliant options?
3. Which risks remain after the recommended option?
4. Which conditions should the board attach to approval?

## Detailed Step-by-Step

1. Write the decision request in one sentence.
2. Summarise relevant filing and record-keeping requirements.
3. Compare manual improvement, phased adoption and full adoption.
4. Assess cost, error risk, authorisation, business continuity and evidence quality.
5. Select a recommendation and state residual risks.
6. Add approval conditions, owner, timeline and post-implementation control test.

## Acceptance Check

- [ ] Decision explicit
- [ ] Options compared fairly
- [ ] Business facts drive recommendation

## Debrief

- What assumption had the greatest effect on your recommendation?
- What evidence would a director, auditor or regulator expect to see?
- What would you verify with qualified legal, tax or sector advice?
