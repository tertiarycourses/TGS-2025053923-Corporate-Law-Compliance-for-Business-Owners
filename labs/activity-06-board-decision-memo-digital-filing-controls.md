# Activity 6: Board Decision Memo: Digital Filing Controls

**Mapping:** K2, A2 / LO2
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Deliverable:** One-page board decision memo.

## Goal

Frame a proportionate board decision that balances legal compliance, control quality, cost, implementation risk and operational benefit.

## Real Case Study

**Case:** Nagore Trading - GST missing-trader-fraud scheme
**Date:** 29 June 2026
**Status:** Key syndicate member convicted and sentenced
**Official source:** [IRAS - Key GST missing-trader-fraud syndicate member sentenced](https://www.iras.gov.sg/news-events/newsroom/key-member-behind-goods-and-services-tax-missing-trader-fraud-syndicate-sentenced-to-six-years%27-imprisonment)

### Published Facts

IRAS reported that Giam Zi Hin, Luke was sentenced to six years' imprisonment for fraudulent trading in a GST missing-trader-fraud scheme involving about S$114 million in fictitious sales. Under his directions, Nagore Trading Pte Ltd used forged invoices from at least 12 local suppliers to create a false appearance of purchases and GST payments. Eight accused persons were charged in the scheme and the proceedings concluded with convictions.

### Exercise Boundary

The case demonstrates that digitisation cannot replace transaction authenticity and due diligence. The board options, system controls and approval conditions are learner recommendations.

## Questions

1. Which controls could test supplier identity, invoice authenticity and transaction substance?
2. What should remain a human approval or exception decision?
3. Which due-diligence evidence should be retained before claiming input tax?
4. Which conditions should a board attach to digital filing automation?

## Detailed Step-by-Step

1. Write the decision request in one sentence.
2. Summarise relevant filing and record-keeping requirements.
3. Compare manual improvement, phased adoption and full adoption.
4. Assess cost, error risk, authorisation, business continuity and evidence quality.
5. Select a recommendation and state residual risks.
6. Add approval conditions, owner, timeline and post-implementation control test.
