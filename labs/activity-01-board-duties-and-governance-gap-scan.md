# Activity 1: Board Duties and Governance Gap Scan

**Mapping:** K1, A1 / LO1  
**Duration:** 25 minutes  
**Deliverable:** One-page governance gap scan and two-minute board briefing.

## Goal

Identify director, record, reporting and register gaps; distinguish tasks that may be outsourced from accountability that remains with directors.

## Scenario

Red Lantern Pte Ltd has a nominee director who signs documents without reading them, an outsourced accountant who is three months behind, and a share transfer that was never updated in Bizfile. The board assumes the service providers carry the legal responsibility.

## Questions

1. Which facts indicate a legal or governance failure?
2. Which director duties are engaged and what evidence is missing?
3. What immediate containment actions are required?
4. What recurring controls would prevent the same gap?

## Detailed Step-by-Step

1. Read the scenario once for the business story and a second time for compliance signals.
2. Create four columns: Requirement, Current fact, Gap/risk, Evidence needed.
3. Map each fact to ACRA director, financial-reporting or register guidance.
4. Classify each response as contain now, correct within deadline, or strengthen for future cycles.
5. Prepare a two-minute board briefing that states the issue, decision, owner and deadline.
6. Peer-review another group's response for unsupported assumptions or missing director challenge.

## Acceptance Check

- [ ] All material facts mapped
- [ ] Director accountability stated correctly
- [ ] Owners/deadlines named

## Debrief

- What assumption had the greatest effect on your recommendation?
- What evidence would a director, auditor or regulator expect to see?
- What would you verify with qualified legal, tax or sector advice?
