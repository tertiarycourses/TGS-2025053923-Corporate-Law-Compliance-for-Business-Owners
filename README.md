## About the course

The course covers Singapore's legal and regulatory framework in a global context, industry-specific business laws and compliance duties, and how to drive decisions and influence business units when regulations change. Each activity is anchored to a documented Singapore ACRA, IRAS, MOM or PDPC case, with the published facts separated from learner-designed recommendations.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Assess the implications of global legal developments and business laws on the organisation's regulatory framework.
- **LO2:** Drive decision-making based on regulatory changes and influence business units to adapt to new laws.

## Topics covered

1. Singapore's Legal and Regulatory Framework in a Global Context
2. Industry-Specific Business Laws and Compliance
3. Business Decision-Making in Response to Regulatory Changes
4. Influencing Business Units to Adapt to New Regulations
